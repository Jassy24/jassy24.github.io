Rotary District 7690


The Reason

I partnered with Xtern Software once again, this time tasked with redesigning a website to modernize how it delivers information to users and align it with current digital standards.

The Process

I began by refining and rebuilding five preapproved webpages to ensure they aligned with the updated design approach. Using these as a foundation, I then expanded the site by creating 16 additional pages, resulting in a cohesive and consistent 21 page experience completed in 30 hours.

The Result

The finished website offered users an intuitive, visually engaging experience that made information easy to access and allows for the brand to have a modern online presence.
