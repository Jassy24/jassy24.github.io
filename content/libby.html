Libby App Redesign


The Reason

As an avid reader, I used the Libby app frequently but found its navigation confusing and its contrast poor. I wanted to simplify the structure and make the experience easier for users of all ages, while keeping its charm intact.

The Process

Research & Discovery

I explored common pain points among readers, noting inconsistent navigation paths and weak contrast.

Design & Accessibility

I introduced a new color palette with a deep primary accent (#671936) and accessible swatches for contrast. I reorganized screens to prioritize Home, Search, and Profile; the areas users interact with most.

Research & Discovery

After sharing the redesign with peers, I received positive reactions about the color choices and layout clarity.

The Result

The redesign created a more welcoming and readable experience. It taught me that accessibility and implicity are often the strongest forms of design, and that even small navigation changes can dramatically improve usability.
