CruxDB Website Redesign


The Reason

CruxDB wanted to modernize their existing landing page, which lacked structure, contrast, and readability. The team’s goal was to make information easier to digest, update the layout to current design standards, and create a stronger first impression for visitors

The Process

Analysis & Planning

I reviewed the live page and identified issues with spacing, font hierarchy, and information flow. The client’s request was to improve readability and update the visual style without changing the site’s structure.

Design & Refinement

Working in Figma, I created two versions (desktop and mobile) that rebalanced text and white space while improving visual hierarchy. I also designed a few simple vector elements that are now featured on the live site.

Feedback & Iteration

The CruxDB team provided quick feedback, noting they were impressed by the turnaround time and the quality of the design. They appreciated that I took feedback well and made thoughtful adjustments with minimal revision.

The Result

The redesigned page delivered a sleek, modern look that aligns with CruxDB’s technical identity. The project demonstrated my ability to deliver polished, high-impact design work under tight timeframes while maintaining strong collaboration and responsiveness to feedback.
