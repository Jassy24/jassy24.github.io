Recipe Platform Redesign


The Reason

This personal project started after struggling to read a recipe I loved on a cluttered site. I wanted to redesign it into something cleaner, structured, and easy to follow, especially for beginner cooks.

The Process

Research & Analysis

I reviewed several recipe websites to see what caused user frustration: small fonts, scattered ingredients, and endless scrolling.

Design & Layout

In Figma, I designed a card-based layout with ingredients on one side and instructions on the other. I used clear typography (Righteous for titles, Roboto for body) and consistent spacing to keep the page light and readable.

Testing & Feedback

Peers mentioned the interface reminded them of YouTube’s simplicity, and they liked the structure and ease of reading.

The Result

The final design reads like a digital recipe card organized, approachable, and visually calm. This project strengthened my understanding of spacing, typography, and balancing creativity with clarity.
