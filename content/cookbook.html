CookBook Project


The Reason

Many beginner cooks struggle with unclear recipes and cluttered websites. I aimed to create a simple, readable recipe platform that reduces confusion and makes cooking approachable.

The Process

I analyzed existing recipe websites, noting what worked and what caused friction. Using Figma, I designed a clean layout, optimized white space, and created consistent typography with Righteous for titles and Roboto for body text. Icons came from Font Awesome, and photos were sourced from Freepik.

The Result

The project strengthened my design fundamentals, from typography and spacing to visual hierarchy. It’s a polished, beginner-friendly recipe platform that balances aesthetics with usability.
