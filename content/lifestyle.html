Green Lifestyle (CSC 442)


The Focus

Help users learn sustainable living through a mobile app that offers challenges, progress tracking, and rewards.


The Process

I conducted interviews and surveys to identify user pain points such as information overload, cost concerns, and desire for social sharing. Designed features included personalized recommendations, gamified rewards, and a clear, user-centric interface.


The Result

A mobile app that encourages engagement, motivates sustainable behaviors, and delivers a clear, personalized experience for users.
