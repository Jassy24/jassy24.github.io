CSC 442 -Green Lifestyle

Focuses on Individuals

  • By creating a mobile application that leverages authentic data for users to consume
  • Help learning and practicing sustainable living
  • Keep users engaged - with challenges, progress tracking and User-centric interface.
  • Rewards to redeem

Need-finding

  • One-on-one interviews with the probable users and surveys
  • Characteristics
  • Age: 20- 27
  • 3 Undergraduates, 3 Graduates and 1 Zero-waste sustainability expert

Findings

  • Overwhelmed with information / general mistrust of information
  • Personalized features/ recommendations is the most sought feature.
  • Cost was one inhibiting factor mentioned by the users; therefore, reward system is another need to keep the users engaged
  • Users also said that if there was a way to showcase their achievements or progress on the social media, that would be nice.
