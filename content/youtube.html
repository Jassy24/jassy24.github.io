YouTube Recreation


The Reason

I wanted to focus on fundamental UX skills with a simplified, functional redesign rather than flashy visuals.

The Process

I built all icons from scratch, structured layouts for clarity, and selected fonts similar to YouTube’s. Antonio for logos and Roboto for interface text. Photos were sourced from Freepik and free online resources.

The Result

This project reinforced my appreciation for clean interfaces, effective use of shapes/colors, and designing experiences that prioritize clarity and usability.
