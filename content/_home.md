Nice to meet you!I am a new designer that has recently found her passion in design. During my time at University, I was involved in multiple projects where I would be asked to overlook the UX/UI design. Being put in this position meant that I would have to create multiple wireframes, prototypes, and conduct UX user interviews for research. Due to this, I am proficient in platforms such as Figma, InDesign, and PowerPoint. A little unknown fact about me is that I was the first early college student at Cumberland Polytechnic to graduate with an associates in Information Technology.

Since I am curious by nature, I am always excited to learn new tools or information that will lead me to become a better designer. In my free time, I enjoy reading, creating magazines, and scrap booking. Currently I am reading The Hobbit, Sense and Sensibility, Dont Make Me Think, and The Design Of Everyday Things. I am also a content creator on Instagram (@jas_small) and used to be a social media ambassador for both Amazon Student Prime and Samsung. I love nature connecting with nature and often find myself studying the trees or the birds around me. Thanks for taking the time to stop by!
