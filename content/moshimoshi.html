Moshi Moshi Boba Cafe


The Reason

This project was created in collaboration with the owners of Moshi Moshi Boba Cafe in Greensboro, NC. My goal was to redesign their website to reflect the cafe's warm and welcoming atmosphere.

The Process

Research & Analysis

I started by reviewing their existing site and analyzing how customers interacted with it. I found that information was scattered, and the site functioned more like a static menu than a full digital storefront. My goal was to create a layout that balanced visual storytelling with clarity.

Design & Layout

To capture Moshi Moshi’s cozy charm, I created a color palette inspired by their shop interior and signature drinks. I added photos of the cafe and reorganized their information into clean and accessible sections that feel natural to browse.

Testing & Feedback

The owners and early viewers shared positive feedback, mentioning that the site looked modern and clean. They especially liked the colors, layout, and how the design maintained the cozy and friendly atmosphere while improving readability.

The Result

The final redesign combines subtle shadows, balanced spacing, and calm tones that create a welcoming digital environment that mirrors the cafe's personality. Each section flows intuitively, from the hero to the menu and gallery, giving users a complete sense of place. This project strengthened my approach to using visual hierarchy and color psychology to build immersive experiences.
