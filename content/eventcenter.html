Event Center (CSC 340)

The Reason

Event discovery is fragmented, ticket purchases are cumbersome, and vendor management is inefficient.

The Purpose

EventCenter centralizes discovery, ticketing, and vendor management in one platform. Features include search & filters, secure payments, real-time notifications, and personalized recommendations.

The Result

An intuitive platform that simplifies the event ecosystem for attendees, organizers, and vendors while enhancing engagement and community connection.
